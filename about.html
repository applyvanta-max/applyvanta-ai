About ApplyVanta.ai

AI interview coaching that keeps trust in the room.

ApplyVanta.ai helps candidates prepare, practice, and improve with transparent AI assistance, visible transcription controls, and clear consent notices.

Preparation should make you stronger, not deceptive.

The product is designed around mock interviews, resume and job description analysis, answer coaching, meeting notes, and feedback reports. AI can help users think clearly, tell stronger stories, and understand weak areas before high-stakes conversations.

ApplyVanta.ai does not support hidden overlays, stealth assistance, screen-share bypass, anti-detection behavior, or cheating workflows. When transcription, recording, or coaching is active, the interface says so clearly.
